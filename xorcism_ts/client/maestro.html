🎭️ MAESTRO — Agentic AI Threat Modeling

MAESTRO (Multi-Agent Environment, Security, Threat, Risk & Outcome) is the Cloud Security Alliance's threat-modeling framework for agentic AI. It decomposes an agentic system into a seven-layer reference architecture and enumerates layer-specific and cross-layer threats. Layer 6 (Security & Compliance, in amber) is a vertical layer cutting across all others; the red column holds cross-layer threats. CSA reference ↗
