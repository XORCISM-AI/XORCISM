📊 SME Cyber Resilience Maturity — ENISA CRA self-check

A faithful replication of ENISA's SME Cyber Resilience Maturity Assessment Model (July 2026). Score 25 questions across five domains on the 1–5 rubric; the tool computes each domain average, the overall maturity band (Basic 1–2.5 · Intermediate 2.6–3.9 · Advanced 4–5) and a prioritised improvement roadmap from the model's Annex B checklist. The domains map to CRA Annex I / Articles 13–14 — but this is a maturity self-check, not a proof of compliance.
