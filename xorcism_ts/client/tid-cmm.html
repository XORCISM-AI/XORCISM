TID-CMM — Threat-Informed Detection

The detection companion to TIR-CMM. TID-CMM (Threat-Informed Detection Capability Maturity Model, tid-cmm.com) measures whether detection is driven by adversary behaviour, whether the telemetry exists to see it, and whether any of it has been proven to work. Eight weighted domains, 58 sub-capabilities scored 0–5 against per-level descriptors (or NA if scoped out). Aligned to MITRE ATT&CK and crosswalked to NIST CSF 2.0 and SOC-CMM.
