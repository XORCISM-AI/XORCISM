Control weight calculator

Not every control matters equally. This is the CapGRC control-weight model: PfC = (T × E × PfO × P) / 100 — control type (T 1–3), scope (E 1–3), the sum of the four security objectives (PfO 0–20) and the % of assets protected (P 0–100), giving a weight of 1–180 banded Faible / Moyen / Élevé. Weights are stored per organisation (P depends on your estate) and feed the Enterprise Risk Score: a heavy control that is implemented earns assurance credit, a heavy control left unimplemented is a weighted gap that costs more.
